# Release notes: mbmealprep.online

Newest first. The same notes appear in the app under About > Release notes.

## 1.3 · 2026-10-14
- About card at the bottom of the Data page: version, release date, links and copyright in one place. It replaces the small-print line that ran along the bottom of every page.
- Installing from matthewbroyer.com: the hub's Install button opens the app with a short install prompt.
- New "page not found" page that matches the app and links back to it.
- Version numbers restart at 1.x with the suite's "Official Web App" label; earlier web-app releases were numbered v5.x. Your saved data, backups and CSV files are unchanged.

## v5.2 · Web app · October 13, 2026 · Sidebar and sharing
- When you install it, the app is now named **Meal Prep** on your computer and phone.
- Navigation moves to a left sidebar on computers (an icon rail on tablets), with Data, About and Setup at the bottom. Phones keep the bottom bar. The logo and name always take you back to Home.
- Share your week: in the Planner, Week at a glance has a Share button that makes an image of your week (meals planned, recipes used and days on target) right on your device. Save it or share it. Recipe names and body details are never on the image.
- New produce and citrus art on the Home banner, the setup welcome and empty pages, and a refreshed logo and app icons.
- The Install card on the Data page now says "Installed on this device" when you are using the installed app.
- Fixed: on small phones the Skip setup button no longer runs off the screen.
- Privacy Policy updated to describe the share image. It is made on your device and nothing is uploaded.
- The version label now reads "v5.2 · Web app". Your saved data, backups and CSV files are unchanged.

## BETA 4.4 · October 9, 2026 · Install as an app
- Install it like an app: on Chrome, Edge and Android, the Data page has an Install button. On iPhone and iPad, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It opens full screen with its own icon.
- After your first visit, the app opens with no signal. Your data was already stored on your device and still is.
- Privacy Policy and Terms updated to describe the small app files your browser now keeps. Your recipes and plans are not sent anywhere.

## BETA 4.3 · October 9, 2026 · Smarter planning
- Browser tabs now read "Meal Prep | page name", for example "Meal Prep | Grocery".
- Auto-plan is smarter: choose which days to fill (skip days you eat out), set a longest cooking time and a weekly grocery budget. It leaves out recipes you gave a thumbs-down, favors thumbs-up recipes, and uses up leftovers first.
- Swap any single meal with one tap, without rebuilding the week. The swap checks the rest of that day so you stay near your targets.
- New week: saves the week you just finished to Past weeks, clears the shopping check marks, and lets you repeat the same meals, auto-plan a fresh week or start empty. Past weeks lets you reuse any saved week in one tap.
- New Prep page: your plan as one cooking session, with batches, containers, what goes in the fridge or freezer by day, ingredients to prep together, and a step-by-step checklist. Pick your prep day.
- Leftovers and freezer log: portions you already cooked are subtracted from the next grocery list, with "use soon" reminders for the fridge.
- Prices and costs: enter what you pay per unit once and see cost per serving and per gram of protein on each recipe, the estimated cost of the week, and the total for the grocery list. Sort recipes by cost, and plan to a weekly budget.
- Pantry check on the grocery list: tick everything you already have in one pass.
- Recipes get a thumbs-up or thumbs-down, and an optional total time in minutes.
- Backups carry all of it. CSV gains "rating" and "minutes" columns and "price", "stock" and "history" rows. Older CSV and JSON backups still import unchanged.
- Privacy Policy and Terms updated. Nothing about how data is handled changed: no accounts, no network connections.
